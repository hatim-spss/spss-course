Lesson 2: Descriptive statistics

Descriptive statistics summarise your data: the average, the lowest and highest values, and how spread out the values are.

What you will learn

  • What the mean, minimum, maximum and standard deviation tell you
  • How to run Descriptives in SPSS
  • How to read the output table

Step 1: Open the Descriptives window

  1. Open your data from Lesson 1.
  2. Click Analyze → Descriptive Statistics → Descriptives.

Step 2: Choose your variables

  1. Click Age and Score, then press the arrow to move them to the right box.
  2. Click OK.
Tip: Do not move Student across. It is only an ID number, so its average means nothing.

Step 3: Read the output

With the Lesson 1 data, SPSS shows:

Variable N Minimum Maximum Mean Std. Deviation
Age32022211
Score36882757

What the columns mean

  • N: how many values SPSS counted.
  • Minimum / Maximum: the lowest and highest values.
  • Mean: the average.
  • Std. Deviation: how far values typically sit from the mean. A bigger number means more spread.

Practice

Add your 3 extra students from Lesson 1 and run Descriptives again. What happens to the mean score?
